Privacy Policy for Dice

Effective Date: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Dice, developed by Benny Thazhutha ("we", "our", or "us"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our application.

Summary: Dice does not require an account or collect direct personal details (like names or emails). However, it displays advertisements via third-party ad networks, which may collect standard device identifiers and usage data to serve ads.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not directly collect, store, or transmit personal identity data (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number). Core functionality—rolling dice and saving preferences—operates entirely on your device.

However, to support free access, the app integrates third-party advertising services that automatically collect technical data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate coarse location
  • App interaction data, diagnostic logs, and ad performance metrics

2. Third-Party Advertising and Services

This app uses third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob) to serve ads. These third parties may collect and process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

For more details on how our primary advertising partner handles your data, please review:

  • Google Ad Policies & Technologies
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Local Storage

App preferences (such as dice count, themes, or sound settings) are saved locally on your Android device. This data is never uploaded to our servers and can be wiped at any time by clearing the application cache/data or uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Dice is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information via ad interactions, please contact us so we can take necessary corrective measures.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
